Account & data deletion

Request deletion of your Kizuki account and associated data, with or without the app.

Developer: Prana codes
App: Kizuki (Android & iOS)
Package name / Bundle ID: com.kizukijapan.app

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email pranacodes@gmail.com from the email address used for your Kizuki account.
  2. Use the subject Kizuki Account deletion and ask us to delete your account and associated data.
  3. We may need to verify ownership. Never send your password.
Email deletion request ↗

This opens your email app. Send the email to submit the request; opening the link does not delete an account.

We aim to handle verified requests within 30 days, subject to legal requirements and permitted extensions.

Delete in the app

Kizuki → Profile → Settings → Delete account. Follow the confirmation steps.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account, profile and synced learner progress.
  • Saved words and notes, reviews, streaks and cloud learning history.
  • Associated support messages, unless legal or security retention is required.

Successful in-app deletion clears local learner state. For an email request, separately clear any app or browser data still on your devices.

Data that may be retained

Apple and Google retain purchase records under their policies. Legally required purchase records may remain for the applicable tax and accounting period. Security records and provider backups may remain under legal requirements and provider retention settings.

For previously collected optional analytics, include an analytics deletion request in your email. Contact us for applicable retention periods.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Account deletion does not cancel a subscription. Cancel through your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings to stop future renewals.
